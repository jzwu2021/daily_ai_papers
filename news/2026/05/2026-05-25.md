# AI News Digest — 2026-05-25

Curated from recent live sources.

## Overview

The past few days of AI news show enterprise deployment maturing along three fronts: coding agents with clearer buyer signals, operational case studies that tie AI use to measurable outcomes, and security workflows that are being reshaped by model-assisted discovery. At the same time, multimodal generation keeps getting more flexible, which expands creative tooling while also raising the stakes for provenance and trust. Overall, the strongest signal is that AI products are being judged less by headline novelty and more by whether they can fit into real production systems.
